⚡ LE Sales — Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026 · App: LE Sales (Android package in.lalitenterprises.sales, web app and iOS app)

Summary. LE Sales is an internal work app of Lalit Enterprises, Ranchi, Jharkhand, used only by its employees. After an employee taps Start day, the app shares that employee's location with Lalit Enterprises — including when the app is closed or the phone is locked — until they tap End day, sign out, or 9 PM. We do not sell or share this data with anyone else and we do not show ads.

Who we are

Lalit Enterprises, an electrical goods distributor in Ranchi, Jharkhand, India ("we", "us"). Contact: +91 70042 21338.

Who can use the app

Only people given an account by Lalit Enterprises (employees, managers and the owner). There is no public sign-up.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, work email, phone number, role and territory, entered by the owner when creating the account.
  • Work records you enter: customer visits, customer names and contacts, quotations, orders, payment collections, follow-up dates and notes.
  • Location at check-in: GPS position and accuracy when you record a customer visit.
  • Location during your work day (Android/iOS app): after you tap Start day, your GPS position about every 3 minutes and when you move about 100 m, including in the background (app closed or screen locked), with time, accuracy, speed and direction. Collection stops when you tap End day, sign out, your account is deactivated, or automatically at 9 PM. No location is collected before you tap Start day.
  • Device information sent with location: battery level and charging state, phone make and model, and app version — used to explain gaps (e.g. phone battery died).
  • In the web version, location is shared only while the app is open.

Why we use it

  • To show the owner and managers where the sales team is during working hours, the route taken that day, and visits made.
  • To plan customer visits, verify field work, follow up quotations and track collections against targets.
  • We do not use the data for advertising, and we do not sell, rent or share it with third parties.

Who can see it

Salespeople see only their own data. The owner and managers of Lalit Enterprises can see the team's records and locations. Access is enforced by sign-in and database security rules.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, Mumbai region asia-south1) and sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. Google acts as our service provider. A copy of your own recent records and unsent location points is kept on your phone so the app works offline.

How long we keep it

Work records and location history are kept for as long as they are needed for Lalit Enterprises' business and accounting purposes. You may ask us to delete your location history or account data by contacting us; we may keep records we are required to keep by law.

Your choices

  • Tap End day at any time to stop location sharing; a persistent notification "Lalit Enterprises — location sharing on" shows whenever it is active.
  • You can turn off the app's location permission in your phone settings (location-based features will then not work).
  • To access, correct or delete your data, call +91 70042 21338.

Children

The app is for adult employees only and is not directed at children.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change and change the effective date above.
